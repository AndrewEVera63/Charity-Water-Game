💧 How to Play
🌍 Mission

Collect clean water drops while avoiding pollution to help bring clean water to communities around the world.

🎮 Gameplay
  • 💧 Water Drops = +10 Points
  • ☣ Pollution = Lose 1 Life
  • ❤️ You begin with 3 Lives
  • 🏆 Reach 500 Points to win!
🕹 Controls
  • 💻 Desktop: Use the ← and → arrow keys.
  • 📱 Mobile: Swipe left or right.

Good luck and have fun! 💛
