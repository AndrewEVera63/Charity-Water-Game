💧 How to Play Water Quest
🌍 Mission

Help bring clean water to communities by collecting water drops while avoiding pollution.

🎮 How to Play
  • 💧 Collect water drops to earn 10 points.
  • ☣ Avoid pollution obstacles or you'll lose one life.
  • ❤️ You start with 3 lives.
  • 🏆 Reach 500 points to win!
🕹 Controls
  • 💻 Desktop: Use the ← → arrow keys.
  • 📱 Mobile: Swipe left or right.
